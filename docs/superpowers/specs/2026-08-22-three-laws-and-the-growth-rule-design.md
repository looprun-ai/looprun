# The two laws and the growth rule — the simplification design

The declared-path teaching is many pages carrying many named laws. Underneath them sit two
laws a six-year-old can hold, and every page is an instance of one of them. This design names
the two, states the growth rule that keeps the set closed, maps every current teaching element
to its law, lists the engine repairs that ride the same batch, and plans the rewrite — as its
own measured change, against the measured baseline.

## 1 · The two laws

```
  1 · EVERY SENTENCE WHERE IT IS READ IN TIME
      a rule about USING a tool is glued to that tool — read at the
      attempt, enforced by a check that decides the call.
      a rule about SPEAKING is hung on the desk's wall — read every
      turn, before anything.
      the drawer question decides between them: on a day the tool never
      leaves the drawer, does the rule still matter? YES → wall. NO → tool.

  2 · NOTHING FROM YOUR OWN HEAD
      a figure, a rule, a choice, a claim about the surface — each has
      exactly one lawful source: a READ, a LAW, or the OPERATOR.
      Composing a question or a sentence OUT OF read facts is lawful;
      inventing the facts is not.
```

Stated precisely:

| law | precise form | enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| where it is read in time | a sentence that is meaningless without a call in flight binds the call: order, arguments, grounding, capability. A sentence that matters with every tool in the drawer binds the reply: restraint, scope, routing. The wall is everything a desk reads every turn — its conduct laws AND the contract's facts. A seam sentence is wall content on the desk that holds its act: the refusal arrives on a call, and the sentence must already be in the room | the check refuses the call; the desk prefix is read every turn; the gate refuses an acting tool with no deciding check |
| nothing from the head | each figure, rule, choice and surface-claim has one lawful source, and the desk's own arithmetic, invention or assumption is never it | the checks where the source is checkable (grounded choices, operator-verbatim values, role reads); the wall's spoken restraint where it is not |

The failure families this campaign measured are all law 2 broken in one channel:

```
  a fee computed at the desk               → a figure from the head
  a total before the choice is settled     → a choice from the head
  "cannot approve above the deposit"       → a rule from the head
  "no tool on this surface does that"      → a surface from the head (falsely)
  an operator's claim taken as a record    → someone else's head taken as a source
  a smaller figure sent without a refusal  → a decision from the head
```

## 2 · The growth rule — the set is closed

```
  A NEW CASE-CLASS NEVER BECOMES A NEW LAW.
  It lands in exactly one of three places:

  MACHINE   a gate or emitter check — ONLY when the obligation is
            EXPLICIT: in what the author declared, or in what the exam
            lists. A check never derives an obligation by inference.
  DATA      an entry the author writes and the engine carries — a seam
            sentence, a choice's terms, a role list
  EXAMPLE   a worked instance of one of the two laws, in an invented
            domain — when the class is a wording shape, not a new duty
```

The decision procedure, asked in order:

```
  1  Is the obligation explicit — in the declaration, or in the exam?
        YES → MACHINE: a check that verifies what is written.
        A check whose obligation is INFERRED is forbidden: the measured
        failure of that shape demanded an order no author could satisfy.
  2  Is it a sentence whose CONTENT is the domain's, with a fixed home?
        YES → DATA: the YAML carries it, the emitter homes it.
  3  Otherwise it is a SHAPE of one of the two laws:
        → EXAMPLE inside the law's existing teaching. Never a new law
          name, never a new page.
```

This campaign, classified retroactively — the rule reproduces every fix that held:

| measured failure | landing | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| an acting tool no check can refuse | machine | the deniable-act and act-without-check verbs — the exam explicitly lists the act |
| a destructive act approved on the author's own words | machine | the disclosure-figures verb over the declared entry |
| an ungrounded boolean choice | machine + data | the choice gate over declared terms |
| a `needs` alias outside the owning desk's lane | machine | the emitter refuses it: the alias, the act and the lane are all declared, and the engine deterministically cannot run the read — measured as a false `empty` refusal |
| a world refusal with no human sentence | data | `contract.seam` entries |
| the order between a read and an act | data + teaching | an authored `onlyAfter` where the author means it — never a demanded one; the repair-channel teaching carries when |
| a required read or act whose result goes unspoken | teaching | the tense teaching; the T loop measures it per subject |
| a fee computed at the desk | example | a clause in an existing wall template |
| a desk-scoped claim where the surface owes one | example | a sentence in an existing template's law |
| a refusal by silent substitution — the ceiling proposed without saying the operator's own figure was refused | example | one clause in the refusal shape already taught: name the figure asked for, the figure available and the difference, say the difference goes outside this system, and put nothing up — measured on two desks (settlement, refund), cured on one by phase T |

What never lands anywhere: a byte ceiling (the bench measures bytes operator-side; no product
field carries a budget), a conduct-spread demand (a law one desk teaches is that desk's own),
and any check whose obligation no declaration and no exam states.

## 4 · The engine repairs riding this batch

Six existing checks keep their demand and get their build fixed, plus one new emitter check.
Every one verifies something EXPLICIT; none derives.

| check | the defect | the fix |
|---|---|---|
| deniable-act | counts a gate as protection without asking whether it can refuse the case's scenario | the check asks the gate's own shape: an `onlyAfter` clears by reading and never denies; a role or state precondition, a choice, an operator value can |
| act-without-check | counts `onlyAfter` as the deciding check | same distinction: owing is not deciding |
| disclosure-figures | accepts `{args.…}` — the model's own words — as a figure, and resolves alternatives ignoring `anyOf` | a figure slot is one over a `needs` alias; `anyOf` widens which read satisfies it |
| seam reader | matches codes by literal only | the reader resolves through the same name lists the other checks use |
| choice gate | substring grounding ("no insurance" grounds `includeInsurance: true`); an omitted argument bypasses the gate | negation-aware term matching over the declared terms; an absent argument on a gated choice refuses |
| approvable | the reference subject opts out | the opt-out closes; the check runs wherever a preset is declared |
| needs-in-lane (NEW) | a `needs` alias naming a read the owning desk's lane does not hold emits cards whose `empty` tense fires with a false reason on every call | the emitter refuses the declaration, naming the alias, the desk and the read |

## 5 · The rewrite plan — the skill, on its own branch

Scope: teaching pages only, on the skill branch. The battery, the templates gate, the triangle
lint and the fingerprint sweep hold before and after.

```
  1  declare.md opens with the two laws; the field map, the walk and
     the factory table become their instruments.
  2  guard-catalog.md §2 presents the six wall templates as six voices
     of law 1's wall carrying law 2's restraint; the ladder stays the
     tool vocabulary.
  3  Every remaining named micro-law is folded into the law it
     instantiates or into a lesson; the only law names the skill
     carries are the two laws and the six template names.
  4  The walk shrinks: every pairing row a gate check now covers
     collapses into "run the gate early — its findings are the walk for
     everything explicit." The walk keeps the judgment rows no check can
     hold.
  5  The said-refusal clause (§2's last example row) lands in the
     refusal shape the templates already teach.
  6  Wording repairs from review: law 2's precise form permits composing
     from read facts; the wall includes the contract facts; a seam
     sentence's home is the desk that holds the act.
```

What the T loop does NOT make removable: the checks (static, free, before any model call), the
drawer question, the templates' four parts, and the repair-channel table phase T teaches.

## 6 · The update strategy — what base, on what branch, accepted how

```
  THE BASE     the CURRENT head of the skill — the state whose subject
               measured 37/40 on the widened slice. The rewrite re-homes
               that content under the two laws; every measured sentence
               survives verbatim wherever the new structure allows.

  THE BRANCH   the rewrite is built on a branch of the skill repo, with
               the pre-rewrite commit recorded in the campaign ledger as
               the baseline. The old skill stays addressable for the A/B.

  THE SPLIT    engine repairs (§4) land on the engine's own branch as
               ordinary check/emitter commits — independently tested,
               not gated by the A/B. Skill content rides the skill
               branch alone.

  ACCEPTANCE   one blind author reads the BRANCH skill against the same
               scaffold: the one-shot score is INFORMATIONAL; the author
               then runs phase T, three rounds at most, self-judged; an
               independent judgment verifies the final declaration on
               the full 40-case slice. The verified score must reach the
               baseline's. Pass -> merge, and the new subject becomes
               the reference. Fail -> the branch does not merge, and the
               failures feed the spec, not the pages.
```

## 7 · What the rule forbids

- A third law, a fourth — and a seventh house template, an eighth: the wall set is closed at
  six templates; new wall content enters as a clause or an example of an existing one.
- A new teaching page per incident — an incident lands as machine, data, or example.
- A check whose obligation no declaration and no exam carries — judgment is taught and
  measured, never faked as machinery.
- Deleting the teaching that measured stable to make room — the budget for growth is the
  closed set itself.
