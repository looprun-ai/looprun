# The three laws and the growth rule — the simplification design

The declared-path teaching is many pages carrying many named laws. Underneath them sit three
laws a six-year-old can hold, and every page is an instance of one of them. This design names
the three, states the growth rule that keeps the set closed, maps every current teaching
element to its law, and plans the rewrite that makes the spine explicit — as its own measured
change, after the certification gate closes.

## 1 · The three laws

```
  1 · THE TOOL     a rule about USING a tool is glued to that tool.
                   Read when the tool is in hand; enforced by a check.

  2 · THE WALL     a rule about SPEAKING is hung on the desk's wall.
                   Read every turn, before anything; worded per desk.

  3 · NOTHING FROM YOUR OWN HEAD
                   everything said or done comes from a READ, a LAW,
                   or the OPERATOR — never invented at the desk.
```

Stated precisely:

| law | precise form | enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| the tool | a sentence that is meaningless without a call in flight binds the call: order, arguments, grounding, capability | the check refuses the call; the gate refuses a missing check |
| the wall | a sentence that matters on a day the tool stays in the drawer binds the reply: restraint, scope, routing | the desk prefix, read every turn; the emitter completes a wall law onto every desk |
| nothing from the head | a figure, a rule, a choice, a claim about the surface — each has exactly one lawful source, and the desk's own arithmetic, invention or assumption is never it | the checks where the source is checkable (grounded choices, operator-verbatim values, role reads); the wall's spoken restraint where it is not |

Law 3 is the unifier. The failure families this campaign measured are all law 3 broken in one
channel:

```
  a fee computed at the desk (610/2)      → a figure from the head
  a total before the choice is settled    → a choice from the head
  "cannot approve above the deposit"      → a rule from the head
  "no tool on this surface does that"     → a surface from the head (falsely)
  an operator's claim taken as a record   → someone else's head taken as a source
```

## 2 · The growth rule — the set is closed

```
  A NEW CASE-CLASS NEVER BECOMES A NEW LAW.
  It lands in exactly one of three places:

  MACHINE   a gate verb or a factory — when the obligation is derivable
            from a schema (the world card, the exam, the declaration)
  DATA      an entry the author writes and the engine carries — a seam
            sentence, a choice's terms, an owner's budget, a role list
  EXAMPLE   a worked instance of one of the three laws, in an invented
            domain — when the class is a wording shape, not a new duty
```

The decision procedure, asked in order:

```
  1  Is the obligation derivable from a schema the gate can read?
        YES → MACHINE (a verb): green means it cannot be skipped.
  2  Is it a sentence whose CONTENT is the domain's, with a fixed home?
        YES → DATA: the YAML carries it, the emitter homes it, a verb
              may demand its presence where the exam makes it load-bearing.
  3  Otherwise it is a SHAPE of one of the three laws:
        → EXAMPLE inside the law's existing teaching — a clause in an
          existing template, a filled skeleton, a lesson. Never a new
          law name, never a new page.
```

This campaign, classified retroactively — the rule reproduces every fix that worked:

| measured failure | landing | what it became |
|---|---|---|
| a required read never ran (17) | machine | the read-order verb |
| a required read answered silently (18, 42's figure) | machine | the spoken-figures verb |
| an act the exam expects refused ran anyway (48) | machine | the deniable-act verb |
| an act the exam expects to run reported nothing (71) | machine | the outcome-tense verb |
| the desks outgrew the owner's byte ceiling (round 7) | machine + data | the budget verb over `promptBudget` |
| a world refusal with no human sentence (45, 60) | data | `contract.seam` entries |
| an ungrounded boolean choice (44's tool half) | machine + data | the choice gate over declared terms |
| a fee computed at the desk (37, 44's mouth half) | example | a clause in an existing wall template |
| a desk-scoped claim where the surface owes one (52) | example | a sentence in an existing template's law |
| a multi-movement ask hiding a movement (42's clause) | example | the composition teaching in the tense family |

The two rows that landed as examples are the two that still rotate between authors — an example
teaches, a machine forces. That boundary is real: content that no schema carries cannot be
forced, only taught, and the measurement is what closes it.

## 4 · The rewrite plan — after the gate, as its own measured change

Scope: teaching pages only. No engine change, no verb change, no data-shape change; the battery,
the templates gate, the triangle lint and the fingerprint sweep hold before and after.

```
  1  declare.md opens with the three laws; the field map, the walk and
     the factory table become their instruments.
  2  guard-catalog.md §2 presents the six wall templates as the six
     voices of the three laws; the ladder stays the tool-law vocabulary.
  3  Every remaining named micro-law is folded into the law it
     instantiates or into a lesson; page count and prompt cost of the
     SKILL itself go down, not up.
  4  Acceptance: one blind author against the rewritten skill, same
     scaffold, same slice — the score must be >= the certified score,
     and authoring time must not grow. Below that, the rewrite does
     not land.
```

## 5 · The T lens — what the measured loop makes removable

With the T phase in the pipeline (author -> run -> self-judge -> fix), any teaching whose only
job is ONE-SHOT perfection is over-built: the loop converges on wording by iteration, and a
static page cannot. Three consequences for the rewrite:

```
  1  THE WALK SHRINKS. Every pairing row a gate verb now covers collapses
     into one sentence: "run the gate early — its findings ARE the walk for
     everything derivable." The walk keeps only the judgment rows no verb
     can hold (which movements an ask composes, which seam rows the cases
     make load-bearing, which shape a wall sentence takes). Eight rows
     become about three, plus the pointer.

  2  TEACH ONLY WHAT THE GATE CANNOT SAY. A page never re-teaches an
     obligation a verb enforces — one pointer at most. The finding text is
     the teaching, delivered at the exact moment it applies, and it cannot
     go stale against the verb it belongs to.

  3  WORDING LESSONS ARE EXAMPLES, NOT DUTIES. The clause shapes that
     measured well (the figure restraint, the surface claim, the split
     movement) stay as filled examples of the three laws; the T loop is
     what guarantees them per subject, because the author who misses one
     meets it in their own transcript and fixes it in minutes.
```

What the T lens does NOT remove: the verbs (static, free, and they run before any model call —
a T iteration costs a run and a judgment; a gate finding costs nothing), the drawer question
(the sorting rule between the first two laws), and the templates' four parts (the format the
templates gate holds).

## 6 · What the rule forbids

- A seventh house law, an eighth, a ninth — the wall set is closed at six templates; new wall
  content enters as a clause or an example of an existing one.
- A new teaching page per incident — an incident lands as machine, data, or example.
- A verb whose obligation no schema carries — judgment is taught and measured, never faked as
  machinery.
- Deleting the teaching that measured stable to make room — the budget for growth is the
  closed set itself.
